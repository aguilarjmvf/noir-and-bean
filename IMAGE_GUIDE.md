# Noir & Bean — Image Guide

All images should feel editorial, tactile, and unhurried. Prefer natural light over studio. Dark tones, warm shadows, and organic textures align with the brand palette (Noir `#0A0A0A`, Parchment `#F5F0E8`, Gold `#C9A96E`). Avoid stock-looking compositions: no smiling people holding mugs at camera, no pure white tabletop flat lays with excessive negative space.

Sources: [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com) · [Pexels](https://pexels.com)

## Image Usage Rules

1. **Overlay rule:** Any photo behind text must have a dark overlay at minimum `bg-black/50`. Hero images use `bg-black/60` or higher.
2. **No white backgrounds:** All product photography should be on dark, neutral, or textured warm surfaces — never pure white.
3. **Consistent color temperature:** Prefer warm-toned photos (golden hour, candlelight, amber interiors). Reject anything with cool blue or harsh flash lighting.
4. **Crop priority:** Portrait cards crop to `aspect-[3/4]`. Post thumbnails crop to `aspect-video`. Always use `object-fit: cover` with `object-position: center`.
5. **Faces and people:** Use sparingly and only in About and Journal. No faces on Shop product images. No overly staged lifestyle shots.
6. **Alt text:** Every image must have a descriptive alt attribute — not keyword-stuffed, but genuinely descriptive for screen readers.
